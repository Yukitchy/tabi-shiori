THE ONE DECISION

Go this morning, not this afternoon

Tokyo to Tonosawa is about two hours. Do it before the rain peaks and the rest of the day takes care of itself.

  • 1Tokyo or Shinagawa → OdawaraTokaido Line, roughly 1 hour 20 minutes. The Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku is the other way in.
  • 2Odawara → Hakone-Yumoto → TonosawaHakone-Tozan Line. Tonosawa is one stop past Yumoto, and the ryokan is a 3 minute walk from the station.
  • 3If the Tozan line stops for rainTake a taxi from Odawara Station straight to the hotel, about 10 to 15 minutes. Do not wait at the station for a train that may not come.
  • 4Check-in and then stop movingEverything below happens under one roof.

The ryokan is the plan

TUESDAY

The storm is gone. Do the loop

Tuesday's forecast is cloudy with some sun. The Hakone loop runs anticlockwise on four different kinds of transport and ends back at your door — train, cable car, ropeway, boat, bus. Note that Tuesday Sep 22 is a national holiday in Japan, so holiday timetables apply and it will be busy.

Up the mountain, over the crater

  • 3Get off at OwakudaniSteam vents, sulphur, and the black eggs — boiled in the hot spring until the shells turn black. ¥500 for four at the Kuro-tamago-kan, sold 9:00–16:40. Local lore says each one adds seven years to your life.

TUESDAY

Across the lake on a pirate ship

It is exactly as silly as it sounds, and the view is genuinely the best on the lake. Covered by the Free Pass.

  • 1Togendai → Motohakone-koThe operator puts the Togendai to Hakone-machi / Motohakone crossing at about 25 to 40 minutes. ¥1,200 one way if you were paying, which you are not.
  • 2Sit outside if it is dryFuji is behind you on the way across, the torii is ahead of you as you come in.
  • 3Hakone Shrine, 10 minutes' walk from the pierCedar avenue up to the shrine. The amulet office is open 8:15–17:00 and the treasure hall takes visitors 9:00–16:00. Opening hours for the grounds themselves are not published — in practice you walk in.
  • 4The torii in the waterThe Peace Torii stands in the lake below the shrine. There is usually a queue to stand in front of it. Worth it.

Two days, no wasted one
